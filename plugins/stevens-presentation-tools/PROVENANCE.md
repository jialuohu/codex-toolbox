# Asset provenance

The Stevens Institute of Technology identifiers, four-point star artwork,
campus photograph, and 2022 PowerPoint source template in this plugin were
supplied by an authorized Stevens user for public redistribution in this
toolbox. Stevens trademarks remain the property of Stevens Institute of
Technology. Inclusion here does not grant permission to alter the marks or use
them outside the applicable Stevens identity standards.

Brand implementation follows, in descending authority:

1. *Stevens Institute of Technology Visual Identity Guide* (2023).
2. *Signage Standards* (May 2025), as confirmation of current marks and color.
3. *Stevens PowerPoint Template Guide* and source template (July 2022), for
   presentation structure.

The positive and negative identifiers were extracted without modification from
the supplied Stevens Keynote theme. The gold raster star and the four CMYK EPS
star variants preserve the supplied official vector artwork; their personal
author field and Adobe XMP document identifiers were replaced with neutral,
same-length metadata values. `campus-view.png` preserves
the supplied pixels from `2022-zoom-background-Castle-Point.jpg` in a PNG
container to avoid media-part collisions with the imported PowerPoint source;
the waterfront campus image includes the official red angle and primary
identifier. Image/text archetypes therefore suppress any additional footer
identifier. The wide-figure archetype covers the otherwise partial embedded
mark with the supplied negative identifier on an opaque Stevens Red tab; it
also suppresses any additional footer identifier.

The redistributed PowerPoint source is a privacy-sanitized Artifact Tool export
of the supplied template. Personal author records, account identifiers,
SharePoint custom XML, comments, and external relationships are excluded. Empty
slide-local placeholders were removed, and the generic `Date` prompt is
`PRESENTATION DAY`; the rendered brand artwork and four source-slide
compositions remain otherwise unchanged.

The generated white, dark, and gallery decks explicitly clear imported author
and comment records before export, so rebuilds preserve the same privacy
boundary as the packaged source.

The bundled Saira Condensed and IBM Plex Sans files are redistributed under the
SIL Open Font License 1.1. Their notices are preserved in the skill assets and
summarized in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.
