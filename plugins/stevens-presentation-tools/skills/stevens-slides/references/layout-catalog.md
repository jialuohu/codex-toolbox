# Layout catalog

Use one archetype per narrative job. Slide numbers refer to every bundled theme
template; slide 1 is the editable index.

| Slide | Archetype | Use for |
|---:|---|---|
| 2 | Text cover | Minimal title, context, presenter, and date |
| 3 | Image cover | Opening where one meaningful image carries the setting |
| 4 | Roadmap | A four-stage agenda, process, or learning progression |
| 5 | Section divider | A deliberate section transition |
| 6 | Key message | One claim or implication that needs space |
| 7 | Title / body | A concise explanation or three short bullets |
| 8 | Two-column | A paired comparison or two complementary arguments |
| 9 | Three-point | Three parallel ideas with equal visual weight |
| 10 | Text / figure left | Evidence first, interpretation second |
| 11 | Figure / text right | Claim first, supporting figure second |
| 12 | Full figure + caption | One large image, diagram, or result with a caption |
| 13 | Chart | Up to three directly labeled series and one takeaway |
| 14 | Results table / comparison | Exact values or option comparison |
| 15 | Metrics | Two or three headline values with definitions |
| 16 | Technical canvas | A small architecture, pipeline, or systems diagram |
| 17 | Quote | A sourced quotation or design principle |
| 18 | Closing / Q&A | Resolution, next action, questions, or contact |

Choose the archetype by narrative role and evidence type, not by object count.
Keep recurring roles on one layout family. If content does not fit, shorten it
or choose another archetype; do not flatten the template into arbitrary boxes.
