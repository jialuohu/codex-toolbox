# Stevens presentation brand system

## Authority

Use the 2023 visual identity guide as the primary authority, the May 2025
signage standards as confirmation, and the July 2022 PowerPoint guide/template
for presentation structure. If they differ, preserve the 2023 color values and
current official marks while retaining useful slide geometry from 2022.

## Typography

- Cover title: Saira Condensed SemiBold, 54 pt, at most two lines.
- Slide title: Saira Condensed SemiBold, 40 pt, at most two lines.
- Subhead: IBM Plex Sans Medium, 20 pt.
- Body: IBM Plex Sans Regular, 18 pt.
- Shorten copy or select a less dense archetype before shrinking.

## Theme semantics

| Theme | Surface and text | Data accents | Identifier |
|---|---|---|---|
| White | White; Dark Gray text | Red, Dark Blue, Gold | Positive |
| Dark | Dark Gray; white/light-gray text | Gold, Orange, Medium Blue | Negative |

Maintain at least 4.5:1 contrast for body text and 3:1 for large text and data
marks. Never put red text on Dark Gray. If a large Stevens Red brand plane is
used, place only white or a documented light treatment on it; never use Dark
Gray text. Do not use color as the only data distinction; add direct labels,
patterns, or shapes.

## Brand gestures

- Use only the official four-point star.
- Use rectilinear angles and chevrons; keep chevron interiors between 120° and
  170°.
- Use solid fills only: no gradients or shadows.
- Keep opacity at or above 85%.
- Use no more than four straight constellation lines on one slide.
- Use at most one identifier per slide and avoid competing stars or angles.
- Do not recolor, redraw, crop, distort, or combine an official identifier.
- Use the primary Stevens identifier by default. Do not invent a unit lockup.

Charts use no more than three primary series. Prefer direct labels and a nearby
text summary of the chart's takeaway.
